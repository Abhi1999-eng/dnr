# DNR Full SEO Audit - 2026-08-12

## Scope

Reviewed the public homepage, product listing/detail pages, service listing/detail pages, blog listing/detail pages, About, Contact, sitemap, robots, metadata helpers, JSON-LD producers, public CMS data flow, and rendered local HTML.

## SEO Expert Audit Fixes

- **Homepage metadata and canonical:** The homepage uses a unique title and description through `createPageMetadata`, with the exact canonical `https://dnrtechnoservices.com`.
- **Homepage FAQs:** Added a visible FAQ section immediately above the footer and matching `FAQPage` JSON-LD. It is scoped to the homepage only.
- **Duplicate organization/site schemas:** Consolidated Organization and WebSite JSON-LD into the root layout. Public pages now emit one Organization and one WebSite schema per rendered page; homepage, About, and Contact no longer add another Organization schema.
- **Organization quality:** The single Organization schema now uses configured business contact and address data, a stable `@id`, de-duplicated contact numbers, and only uses social URLs for `sameAs` when real social links are configured.
- **Products canonicals:** `/products` and all product details use their route-specific canonicals; no fragment (`#products`) is used in a canonical URL.
- **Breadcrumb JSON-LD:** Confirmed on product, service, and blog listing/detail pages, plus About and Contact.
- **Description cleanup:** Metadata descriptions normalize repeated dots and produce sentence/word-safe text without generated dot runs. Homepage coverage descriptions are also normalized before crossing the client boundary.
- **Blog canonical:** Blog detail pages now always canonicalize to their own `/blog/[slug]` URL rather than a manually entered, potentially mismatched canonical value.
- **Dummy testimonials:** Obvious placeholder testimonials are filtered from the public data response. A dummy CMS testimonials subtitle is replaced with production-safe copy at render time.
- **AI crawler guidance:** Added `public/llms.txt`, served at `/llms.txt`.

## Additional Self-Audit Fixes

- **Duplicate H1s in blog articles:** The page title remains the single H1. Markdown body headings are offset to start at H2.
- **B2B schema alignment:** Product detail pages use WebPage with a Service `mainEntity`, BreadcrumbList, and visible FAQPage schema. They do not emit Product merchant-listing data, prices, offers, availability, ratings, reviews, shipping, or return-policy properties.
- **Internal link checks:** Verified public links discovered from sitemap pages return successfully locally.
- **Sitemap and robots:** `sitemap.xml` includes public static, product, active service, and published blog URLs. `robots.txt` allows public pages, blocks `/admin` and `/api/`, and advertises the sitemap.

## Content-Owned Recommendations

- Remove the placeholder testimonial and subtitle from the CMS database rather than relying on the public safeguard. Add only genuine, permissioned client feedback.
- Review short product, service, and blog excerpts in the CMS for complete sentences, specific applications, and unique search intent.
- Upload a dedicated 1200x630 Open Graph image for the homepage and major campaign pages instead of relying on a logo or a product image.
- Add page-specific FAQs only when they are supported by real technical or service information.
- Keep canonical URL values in the blog admin UI aligned with the public route if the field continues to be used for editorial reference; front-end canonical output intentionally uses the route URL.
